# Information Systems Evolution

*Synthesised from [Sapiens Part 2](../books/sapiens/part-2-agricultural-revolution.md).*

## Why Writing Was Invented for Data, Not Literature

The biological human brain is poorly suited for storing large volumes of mathematical and transactional records. As agricultural empires grew, two bottlenecks emerged:

1. **Capacity:** No single person could memorise the tribute owed by tens of thousands of households.
2. **Retrieval & Indexing:** Even once clay tablets or quipus recorded data, empires needed catalogues, taxonomies, and trained scribes to locate and reconcile records.

## Key Insight

Every leap in social scale required a corresponding leap in data representation and retrieval:

- **Oral culture:** Narrative and mythic compression (easy to remember, low precision).
- **Early bureaucratic scripts (Sumerian cuneiform, Andean quipu):** Tabular ledgers and schemas (high precision, narrow domain).
- **Full scripts & Arabic numerals:** Composable general-purpose symbolic systems.
