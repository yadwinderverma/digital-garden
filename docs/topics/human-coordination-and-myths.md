# Shared Myths & Large-Scale Coordination

*Synthesised from reading [Sapiens](../books/sapiens/index.md) and research notes.*

## Core Idea

Human cooperation scales along two distinct regimes separated by **Dunbar's number (~150 individuals)**:

| Regime | Group Size | Primary Mechanism | Failure Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intimate / Tribal** | < 150 | Direct interpersonal knowledge, reputation, gossip | Factional splits when group outgrows personal trust |
| **Institutional / Mythic** | > 150 to millions | Shared fictions, protocols, laws, currencies, charters | Loss of belief in the shared standard or bureaucratic rigidity |

## Three Orders of Reality

1. **Objective reality:** Things that exist independently of human consciousness (rivers, gravity, radioactivity).
2. **Subjective reality:** Things that depend on a single individual's consciousness (an imaginary friend, personal pain).
3. **Intersubjective reality:** Things that exist within the communication network linking the subjective consciousness of many individuals (money, borders, governance rules). Changing your own mind does nothing; changing everyone's mind changes the reality immediately.
