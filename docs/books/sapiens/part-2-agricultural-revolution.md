# Part 2: The Agricultural Revolution

## Key Takeaways

- **History's Biggest Fraud:** Rather than humans domesticating wheat, wheat domesticated *Homo sapiens*. Foragers worked fewer hours, ate a more varied diet, and suffered fewer infectious diseases and repetitive stress injuries than early farmers.
- **Evolutionary Success vs. Individual Suffering:** Evolution measures success strictly by the number of DNA copies, not quality of life. Agriculture multiplied human populations exponentially while lowering the average individual's nutrition and leisure.
- **The Luxury Trap:** Small conveniences (settling near fields, storing grain) ratcheted up population density. Once the population grew, returning to foraging was impossible without mass starvation; luxuries became necessities that created new obligations.
- **The Birth of Data Processing (Writing & Bureaucracy):** Human brains evolved to store botanical, social, and topographical memories, not thousands of tax receipts and grain inventories. Sumerian cuneiform began as partial scripts for accounting, not poetry.

!!! important "Parallel to Modern Engineering"
    The "Luxury Trap" mirrors platform and infrastructure complexity: adopting a tool to solve an immediate bottleneck increases system capacity, which invites higher load and tighter coupling until you can no longer operate without maintaining that layer.
