# Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind

**Author:** Yuval Noah Harari  
**Status:** Reading (1-month loan)

## Core Thesis

Homo sapiens came to dominate the planet because it is the only animal capable of cooperating flexibly in very large numbers. That large-scale cooperation rests on our unique ability to believe in shared fictions: intersubjective realities such as money, nations, corporations, human rights, and laws.

## Parts & Chapter Notes

1. **[Part 1: The Cognitive Revolution](part-1-cognitive-revolution.md)** (c. 70,000 BCE):
    - **Ch 1: An Animal of No Significance:** Sibling human species, the energy cost of jumbo brains, premature birth, fire/cooking, and the hasty jump to the top of the food chain.
    - **Ch 2: The Tree of Knowledge:** The three levels of language (objective, gossip, fictive), Dunbar's number (~150), the Legend of Peugeot, and cultural evolution bypassing DNA.
    - **Ch 3: A Day in the Life of Adam and Eve:** Stone Age evolutionary psychology ("gorging gene", ancient communes), the original affluent society, animism, Sungir burials, and the Aché people.
    - **Ch 4: The Flood:** The First Wave of extinction across Australia (diprotodon, giant kangaroos), the Americas (giant ground sloths, sabre-tooth cats), and Wrangel Island mammoths.
2. **[Part 2: The Agricultural Revolution](part-2-agricultural-revolution.md)** (c. 10,000 BCE):
    - **Ch 5: History's Biggest Fraud:** How wheat domesticated *Sapiens*, evolutionary success (DNA copies) vs. individual suffering, the Luxury Trap, Göbekli Tepe, and animal domestication.
    - *Chapters 6–8 (upcoming):* Building Pyramids, Memory Overload (writing & numbers), and Imagined Hierarchies.
3. **Part 3: The Unification of Humankind** *(upcoming)*: Money, empires, and universal religions as global integrators.
4. **Part 4: The Scientific Revolution** *(upcoming)*: The discovery of ignorance, capitalism, empire, and industrial transformation.
