# Deep Work: Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World

**Author:** Cal Newport  
**Status:** Listened

## Core Thesis

In an increasingly distracted, always-connected knowledge economy, the ability to focus without distraction on cognitively demanding tasks (**Deep Work**) is becoming simultaneously rarer and more valuable. The few who deliberately cultivate this skill and build their working lives around it will thrive, while those trapped in constant busyness and **Shallow Work** will struggle to produce meaningful value.

## Deep Work vs. Shallow Work

- **Deep Work:** Professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit. These efforts create new value, improve your skill, and are hard to replicate.
- **Shallow Work:** Non-cognitively demanding, logistical-style tasks, often performed while distracted. These efforts tend not to create much new value in the world and are easy to replicate.

**The Law of Productivity:**

> **High-Quality Work Produced = (Time Spent) × (Intensity of Focus)**
