# Escaping the Build Trap: How Effective Product Management Creates Real Value

**Author:** Melissa Perri  
**Status:** Listened

## Core Thesis

Most software companies fall into the **build trap**: they get stuck measuring success by **outputs** (how many features, lines of code, or releases they ship) rather than **outcomes** (actual value delivered to customers and measurable results for the business). Escaping the trap requires moving from a project-delivery mindset to a **product-led organisation**, anchored by empowered product managers, clear strategic intent, disciplined experimentation (the Product Kata), and aligned incentives.

## Parts & Chapter Notes

1. **[Part I & II: The Build Trap and the Role of the Product Manager](part-1-build-trap-and-pm-role.md)**
    - **Ch 1: The Value Exchange System:** Outputs vs. outcomes, the build trap, the Value Exchange System loop, and meeting Marquetly.
    - **Ch 2: Projects vs. Products:** Cost-centre IT vs. value-creating software, services vs. products vs. platforms, Uber vs. taxis, and Netflix's evolution.
    - **Ch 3: The Known-Unknowns:** Navigating uncertainty, why software is not bridge construction, and why fixed feature roadmaps fail.
    - **Ch 4: The Bad Product Manager Archetypes:** The Mini-CEO, the Waiter, and the Former Project Manager.
    - **Ch 5: The Great Product Manager:** Owning the "why" and the "what", leading through influence and data, and balancing tactical, operational, and strategic work.
    - **Ch 6: The Product Manager Career Path & Organising Teams:** From Associate PM to CPO, structuring teams around value streams instead of components, and Conway's Law.
2. **[Part III & IV: Strategy and the Product Management Process](part-2-strategy-and-process.md)**
    - **Ch 7: What Is Strategy?:** Strategy as a decision framework rather than a plan, Prussian *Auftragstaktik*, and closing the Knowledge, Alignment, and Effects gaps.
    - **Ch 8: Strategic Intents:** Identifying the business's biggest challenges and giving teams clear goals without dictating solutions.
    - **Ch 9: Product Vision and Product Initiatives:** Crafting a 3-to-5-year North Star, Amazon's working-backwards press release, and framing initiatives around problems.
    - **Ch 10: Strategy Deployment:** Hoshin Kanri, the Strategic Framework cascade (Vision, Strategic Intents, Product Initiatives, Options), and backbriefing.
    - **Ch 11: The Product Kata:** Adapting Mike Rother's *Toyota Kata* into a repeatable scientific loop for product discovery and delivery.
    - **Ch 12: Understanding Direction and Setting Success Metrics:** Pirate Metrics (AARRR), Google's HEART framework, leading vs. lagging indicators, and avoiding vanity metrics.
    - **Ch 13: Problem Exploration:** Generative user research, uncovering root causes behind feature requests, and getting out of the building.
    - **Ch 14: Solution Exploration:** Validating ideas cheaply with Concierge MVPs (Groupon), Wizard of Oz tests (Zappos), prototypes, and smoke tests.
    - **Ch 15: Building and Optimising Your Product:** Agile in service of outcomes, continuous discovery and delivery, and knowing when to persevere, pivot, or kill a feature.
3. **[Part V: The Product-Led Organization](part-3-product-led-organization.md)**
    - **Ch 16: Outcome-Focused Companies:** Product-led vs. sales-led, visionary-led, and technology-led organisations, and building a culture of learning.
    - **Ch 17: Rewards and Incentives:** Why rewarding on-time shipping breeds feature factories, celebrating validated learning, and aligning promotions with outcomes.
    - **Ch 18: Budgeting and Finance:** Ditching annual project budgets for venture-style incremental funding, and navigating CapEx vs. OpEx traps.
    - **Ch 19: Customer-Centricity and Product Operations:** Democratising customer insight across silos and scaling product excellence through Product Operations.
