# Atomic Habits: An Easy & Proven Way to Build Good Habits & Break Bad Ones

**Author:** James Clear  
**Status:** Listened

## Core Thesis

Remarkable results do not come from massive, overnight transformations; they come from the compound interest of tiny, 1% daily improvements embedded in better systems and a stronger identity. By engineering your environment and behaviour around the Four Laws of Behaviour Change (Make It Obvious, Make It Attractive, Make It Easy, and Make It Satisfying), good habits become inevitable and bad habits become impractical.
