# Research & Reading Notes

Welcome to my personal knowledge base. This site collects synthesised notes from books, deep-dive articles, and AI research conversations so key insights remain searchable long after a book is returned or a chat session ends.

## How This Wiki is Organised

- **[Books](books/index.md):** Chapter-by-chapter takeaways, mental models, and quotes from books I am reading.
- **[Topics](topics/index.md):** High-level thematic articles that synthesise ideas across multiple sources and conversations.
- **[Tags](tags.md):** Cross-cutting classification labels (such as `anthropology`, `coordination`, `history`, `systems-thinking`) to connect ideas across books and articles.
